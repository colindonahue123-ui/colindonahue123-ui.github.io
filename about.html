About Me

I'm a data engineer and econometrician based in San Francisco, specializing in building data infrastructure for antitrust and M&A analysis. Over the last four years I've designed and maintained pipelines that ingest, clean, and harmonize large-scale datasets from some of the world's biggest companies — producing outputs submitted to the FTC and DOJ.

Outside of work, I love to travel, sail competitively, scuba dive, make short films and documentaries, and take care of honeybees. I'm also developing my first web application — a tool that helps renters and travelers identify neighborhoods that minimize commutes between their most frequented locations.
