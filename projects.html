MapME
Neighborhood intelligence for renters and travelers

A data warehouse of geospatial and economic characteristics across 100,000+ census blocks in every major U.S. city, covering crime, climate, public health, noise, business density, demographics, and more. The pipeline powers interactive maps that help users find their ideal neighborhood by minimizing commute times to frequented locations and scoring census blocks against a set of personal preferences. The result helps people visualize key aspects of their neighborhood to make an informed decision about where to rent.

Key Features
  • Geospatial pipeline ingesting Census, crime, climate, public health, noise, and business data at the census block level
  • Commute minimization engine that ranks neighborhoods by total travel time across a user's most frequented locations
  • Weighted preference scoring that ranks census blocks by how well they match user-defined characteristics
  • Interactive choropleth maps that make complex real estate and economic data intuitive and visual
